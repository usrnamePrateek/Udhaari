Udhaari Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Udhaari is a personal app for keeping a shared record of money lent and borrowed between people. This page explains what data it stores and why.

What we collect

  • Account details: when you sign in with Google or an email link, we receive your email address, your name and your profile picture. We use these only to identify you and to show your name to people you share a ledger with.
  • Ledger data: the people you add, and the amounts, dates and notes you enter.
  • Change history: who created, edited or deleted each entry, and when.

Who can see your data

A ledger and its entries are visible only to its members: you, and the person who accepted your invite. Nobody else can read it, and access is enforced by the database itself. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for any other purpose.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase, our database and login provider. A copy of your ledgers is also cached on your device so the app works offline.

Google user data

Udhaari requests only your basic Google profile (name, email and profile picture) to sign you in. It does not access your Gmail, contacts, Drive or any other Google data. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

You can delete an entry at any time from within the app. To delete your account and all of your data, email the address below and it will be removed.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for Udhaari.
